## Key strategic findings
1. ~75 of ~95 submissions are agent-infrastructure of some kind. The field converged hard.
2. Strongest competitors (AgentHub, AgentCourt, ClawMind, Provus) are **on mainnet with deep multi-component 0G integration** — the bar for "technical depth" is high.
3. Consumer lane is underbuilt but **no longer empty**; ZeroViza + Compass (both ~8.0, both mainnet) already own the "privacy-preserving AI for vulnerable people" angle.
4. Genuinely untouched verticals across all 95: **personal wellbeing/mental health**, **elder life-story preservation**, and — most importantly — **anything that grows 0G's own economy rather than just using it as storage.**
5. 0G's historical winners (AInfluencer, Warriors AI-rena at ETHGlobal Cannes) were **economic engines for the network**, not databases on top of it. That is the real bar.
